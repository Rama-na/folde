A 5 MB limit is not 5 MB of files

Mail servers measure the encoded message, and encoding inflates every attachment by about 37% before headers are counted. A 5 MB cap is really about 3.5 MB of files.

It is why a carefully-trimmed 4.7 MB email still bounces. On the wire it weighs 6.4 MB.

Everything here happens on your device. Your documents are never uploaded.
